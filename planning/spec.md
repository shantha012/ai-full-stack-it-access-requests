## Open questions

- **Throughput:** requirements describe 200–400 ordinary daily messages, roughly 2,000-message bursts and variable model capacity, but specify no latency target, concurrency limit or retry schedule; these remain documented design assumptions.
- **Shared operation:** coherent overlapping processing is required, but storage, locking and supported deployment topology are unspecified; choose and document a coordination model during planning.
- **Settings and policy identity:** refresh and provenance are required, but relevant-setting fingerprints, policy identifiers and refresh commands are not defined.
- **Failure and semantic validation:** the schema permits nullable date strings without format constraints, while policy requires YYYY-MM-DD and unresolved relative dates; policy supplies semantics, but provider-specific completion handling remains open until a provider is selected.
- **Accuracy comparison:** expected facts must come from source and policy, while exact-match counting, denominator rules, normalization and free-text equivalence must be specified in accuracy.md; the files do not select those rules.
- **Override detection:** routing and evidence are specified, but exhaustive detection criteria are not; supported detection behavior needs explicit examples and tests.
- **Withdrawal precedence:** policy order resolves withdrawals with ID/date before ordinary missing-field clarification, but contradictory withdrawal facts and issue wording need explicit expected fixtures.
- **Source order:** later batches can replace contents, but no authoritative version-order key is specified for overlapping different revisions of one filename.
- **Capture and submission:** CHALLENGE.md delegates capture and Classroom instructions to the facilitator; prerequisites confirm Entire is enabled, while final packaging and Classroom destination depend on those instructions.

Research used the four brief files and messages IT-001, IT-007, IT-010 and IT-014; no provider or external standard was selected for additional documentation research.
